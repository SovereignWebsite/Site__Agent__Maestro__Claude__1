# Moving account A's agents to other accounts (25 Nov 2026)

At Dinis's word in cl-1.maestro's session (formerly a.maestro): account A (cl-1) is at 90% of its week. The active teams move to other accounts, wind-down first. **Each agent's own area on its Site__Agent__* site is the brief:** a new session reads it and carries on. This page only says who moves where and in what order.

## The handover, per agent

1. **Wind down (on A).** Finish or park the current piece, then bring the area current:
   - **agent.json:** state "handing over", waiting_on, updated;
   - **now.md:** what's open, with links;
   - **restart.md:** how a new session takes over: repositories to attach, wake schedule, first steps;
   - **learnings.md.**
2. **Record what the new session needs, without values:**
   - vault keys and lane keys you hold, by id only;
   - the repositories you push to;
   - the scheduled wakes you have (name and schedule only);
   - the blockers waiting on Dinis.

   Push it, and send cl-1.maestro one line: "ready to hand over" and the link to your restart.md.
3. **Stop:** start no new work. Your wakes are disabled once the new session confirms it is running.
4. **Start (on the target account).** The target maestro starts a fresh session at Dinis's word in its own session, from the agent's restart.md, with the new id `<account>.<role>@<project>.dinis`. The new session:
   - makes its own key (the home's tools/setup-signing.sh, or the repository's own START.md), and the old key is marked superseded;
   - asks for any vault key it needs, sealed to its new key;
   - confirms to its maestro and to cl-1.maestro.
